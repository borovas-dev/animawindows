# animawindows privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-17_

animawindows is a free, open-source app. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or telemetry, and its developer runs no servers that receive your data.

## Data stored on your device

Watch history, favourites, downloads, settings and search history are stored only in the app's local folder on your PC. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Backups are files you export yourself.

## Network requests

To work, the app connects directly from your PC to:

- the anime sources you search and play, and the video hosts they use;
- AniSkip (`api.aniskip.com`) to fetch opening and ending times;
- GitHub (`github.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com`) to check for updates and refresh the source list.

These services receive your IP address and the requests needed to load content, under their own privacy policies.

## Optional tracking (AniList, MyAnimeList)

If you connect AniList or MyAnimeList, the app signs in through your browser, stores the access token in Windows Credential Locker on your PC, and sends your episode progress to that service. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the token.
